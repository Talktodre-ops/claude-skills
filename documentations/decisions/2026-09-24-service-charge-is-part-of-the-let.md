# Service charge is part of the let's money

Date: 2026-09-24. Status: accepted. Source: the Home review. Dre: owners who
have a caution fee "might simply just add it as service charge, which is what
we have now".

## Decision

A tenancy carries a service charge per rent cycle, copied from the agreement
and from renewals and settable when an owner adds or edits a tenancy. The
schedule charges rent plus service charge; each period records its service
charge part, so receipts itemize both. A figure that includes service charge is
never called rent on any screen, email or receipt. Existing tenancies have none
and behave exactly as before.

Caution fees stay out until they are designed across the listing, the offer,
the marketplace detail and the let together.

## Why

The offer tells a tenant to pay rent plus service charge to move in. The
tenancy dropped the service charge, so a tenant who paid what the offer said
would have seen the difference as credit, and the owner's roll would have
disagreed with the agreement from the first day.

## Revisit when

Caution fees or other one off charges are designed; they need their own line
rather than riding on the cycle.
